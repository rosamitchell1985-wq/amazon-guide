Chapter 01 / The basics

Start with the Australian storefront.

If you’re shopping from Australia, Amazon.com.au is the local storefront to check first. You can browse by category, search for a specific item and compare offers from different sellers.

Before placing an order, make sure your delivery address is correct. Product availability, delivery eligibility, shipping charges and estimated arrival dates can vary by postcode, item and seller.

Go to Amazon.com.au

Open the Australian website directly in your browser or use the official Amazon app.

Search for a product

Use a clear search term, then refine with filters that match your needs.

Check the listing

Review size, model, condition, seller, delivery estimate and returns information.

Review at checkout

Confirm the total in Australian dollars, shipping costs and delivery address before paying.

A helpful habit

Save the order confirmation and check who is selling and dispatching the item. Marketplace listings can have different fulfilment arrangements.

Chapter 02 / Smart buying

Compare more than the price tag.

A cheaper listed price is not always the better overall deal. Compare the total checkout cost, delivery timing, specifications and return conditions. Look closely at whether the product is new, used or refurbished.

  • Check the exact model: dimensions, compatibility, inclusions and colour can differ between variants.
  • Read a range of reviews: prioritise detailed feedback about the feature you care about; look at recent reviews and common issues.
  • Review seller details: identify the business selling the product and whether Amazon or the seller handles fulfilment.
  • Look at delivery costs: estimated timing and fees may change after you enter your postcode.
  • Compare elsewhere: a quick check against reputable Australian retailers can help you judge value.

Watch for unusual listings

Be cautious with extremely low prices, unclear descriptions or requests to pay outside the Amazon checkout. Do not send a bank transfer or gift-card payment to a seller who contacts you separately.

Chapter 03 / From cart to doorstep

Delivery options, explained.

Delivery choices depend on your address, the product and who fulfils the order. Amazon may show different shipping speeds, while some listings are fulfilled by third-party sellers. The estimated delivery date shown at checkout is the best place to check your particular order.

What about Amazon Prime?

Prime is an optional paid membership that can include delivery benefits on eligible products, along with other membership features. Eligibility, geographic availability, terms and membership prices can change, so review the current details on Amazon’s Prime page before subscribing.

Chapter 04 / After you order

Track a parcel or handle a return.

To check the progress of a recent purchase, sign in through the official Amazon website or app and open Your Orders. Choose the relevant item to view the delivery information or available order actions.

How to start a return

Open Your Orders

Find the item you would like to return in your Amazon account.

Select the return or problem option

If available, follow the prompts and choose the most accurate reason.

Check your return instructions

Review the available return method, deadline and any costs shown for that item.

Keep your receipt or label

Retain any confirmation and tracking information until the matter is resolved.

Change-of-mind eligibility depends on Amazon’s applicable policy, the item and seller. Some categories have exceptions. Faulty or misdescribed goods are a separate issue and may be covered by consumer guarantees regardless of a store’s voluntary change-of-mind window.

Need help with an order?

Use the customer service options within your signed-in Amazon account. Avoid telephone numbers from pop-up ads or unofficial search listings.

Chapter 05 / Important in Australia

Know the difference between returns policy and consumer law.

Australia’s consumer guarantees provide protections for qualifying purchases. Goods generally must be of acceptable quality, match their description and be fit for their stated purpose. If a product does not meet those guarantees, a repair, replacement or refund may be appropriate depending on the issue.

A change-of-mind return is different: consumer law does not usually require a business to accept a return simply because you no longer want an item. A retailer may voluntarily offer change-of-mind returns with its own conditions.

For a faulty product, a seller cannot simply remove your statutory rights with an arbitrary 'no refunds' statement. See the ACCC consumer guarantees guide and the ACCC guide to remedies for current details.

Important distinction

This is general consumer information, not legal advice. Your rights and the available solution depend on the facts, seller and circumstances of the purchase.

Chapter 06 / Safer shopping

Keep your account and payments secure.

Scammers sometimes copy familiar retail branding to send messages about fake missed deliveries, failed payments or account problems. Even a realistic-looking email or SMS may be fraudulent.

  • Open the official app or type amazon.com.au into your browser rather than following a suspicious message link.
  • Use a unique password and enable two-step verification where available.
  • Never share your password, one-time security code or payment details in response to an unexpected call or message.
  • Review order activity within your account if a message claims a purchase has been made.
  • Never grant remote access to your computer to someone claiming to help with a delivery or refund.

If you think you entered information on a fake site, change your password using the official website and contact your payment provider promptly if financial details were exposed.

You asked, we answered

Frequently asked questions.

Quick, practical answers to questions Australian online shoppers often have.

Is Amazon.com.au the Australian Amazon website?

Yes. Amazon.com.au is the local Australian storefront. Product availability, sellers and delivery options can vary by listing and delivery location.

How do I track my Amazon order?

Sign in through Amazon.com.au or the official app, visit Your Orders, and select the item to review its status and available tracking information.

Does Prime mean free next-day delivery on everything?

No. Prime delivery benefits are subject to product and location eligibility, and not every order qualifies for the same shipping speed. Check the listing and checkout estimate.

Can I return an item if I change my mind?

Possibly, if your purchase is covered by an applicable change-of-mind policy. The item, seller and condition matter. Your statutory rights for faulty goods are separate.

What if a product arrives faulty?

Contact the seller through the official order or customer-service process. Qualifying purchases may be protected by the Australian Consumer Law, which can provide rights to a remedy depending on the circumstances.

Where should I get real Amazon customer support?

Use the Customer Service or Help options from Amazon.com.au or within the official app. This independent article is not an Amazon support channel.
